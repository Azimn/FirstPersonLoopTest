# Research Sources and Design Rationale Index

This repository keeps an explicit evidence trail for design decisions that are materially influenced by published research or prior agent systems.

The source notes are **not** claims that Subjective Character Loop reproduces human neurobiology, demonstrates consciousness, or establishes the truth of any single cognitive theory. Sources are used to motivate testable design choices and to preserve why those choices were made.

## Maintenance convention

For each new research-influenced architectural change, record:

1. The design decision.
2. The academic or technical source that motivated it.
3. The limited claim the source actually supports.
4. What the source does not establish.
5. The version in which the decision entered the prototype.

Prefer peer-reviewed primary studies, meta-analyses, systematic reviews, and authoritative scholarly reviews. Engineering precedents may also be included, but should be identified as engineering precedents rather than biological evidence.
